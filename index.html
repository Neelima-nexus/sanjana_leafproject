LEARN WHAT TO NOTICE

Common leaf signs

These signs can have several causes. Use them as prompts for a closer look, not as a diagnosis.

COLOR CHANGE

Yellowing leaves

Check watering, drainage, light, and whether older or newer leaves are affected.

SPOTTING

Brown spots

Note the spot shape, whether it spreads, and if nearby leaves show the same pattern.

SURFACE DAMAGE

Holes or chewed edges

Check both sides of the leaf and inspect nearby foliage for insects.

ABOUT LEAFWISE

A thoughtful companion
for healthier plants.

Leafwise helps gardeners and growers inspect leaf photos, understand possible concerns, and plan practical next steps. It brings a clear scan workflow and plant-care conversations into one place.

🌱

Our purpose

Make plant-care information easier to understand. A leaf photo is a starting point for observation, not the whole story of a plant’s health.

🔍

A careful approach

Photos are checked for a clear plant-leaf subject before assessment. Results explain visible evidence, possible alternatives, and uncertainty. Expert review is recommended when needed.

💬

Meet LeafBuddy

LeafBuddy is the floating plant-care assistant. Groq powers visual assessments, care plans, photo comparison, weather guidance, and follow-up conversations.

🪴

A continuing plant story

Keep individual plants, compare scans, follow a seven-day care checklist, and export an assessment report. Plant records and photo previews are stored in this browser.

Useful answers. Honest limits.

AI can mistake a symptom, plant type, or image detail. Leafwise presents possibilities and visual severity estimates, rather than promising a confirmed diagnosis or 100% accuracy. If symptoms spread or the plant is valuable, consult a local plant specialist.

For AI requests, the selected leaf photo or question is sent directly from this page to Groq. The Groq API key is stored inside this index.html file, so keep the file private or use a restricted key.
